# Code of conduct

Project discussions should remain technical, respectful, and useful.

Participants should:

- criticize ideas, calculations, code, and evidence rather than people;
- provide enough detail for claims and bug reports to be checked;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and
  deliberate disruption;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- clearly distinguish measured facts, simulation results, assumptions, and
  speculation.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive content and may restrict
participation when necessary to keep the project usable.
